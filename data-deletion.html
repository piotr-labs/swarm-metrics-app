Data deletion

Last updated: 8 November 2026

These private tools store data only about the owner's own accounts and posts. To delete it:

  1. Remove the app's access: in Facebook, Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → remove "Swarm Publisher" or "Swarm Metrics"; in Google, myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Email socialswarm.ops@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the account or Page name. Stored tokens, analytics and publishing records for that account are deleted within 30 days and the deletion is confirmed by email.
