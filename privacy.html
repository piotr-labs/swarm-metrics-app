Privacy policy: Swarm Metrics and Swarm Publisher

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Swarm Metrics and Swarm Publisher are private tools. Their only user is the owner, who signs in with the owner's own Google, Facebook and Instagram accounts. They collect no data from anyone else.

Data accessed

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): analytics reports of the owner's channels (read-only scope yt-analytics.readonly); the channel's own ID and title (youtube.readonly); uploading and updating the owner's own videos (youtube.upload); adding caption tracks and managing titles and playlists of the owner's own videos (youtube.force-ssl). These tools use YouTube API Services. By using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta Platform): the list of the owner's Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts, insights of their posts (read-only), and publishing the owner's own videos (Reels) to them. No data about followers, commenters or any other person is requested. No messages are read or sent.

Use

Only to publish the owner's own content and to analyse how it performs. Not for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

On the owner's private computer. Access tokens are held in memory; refresh and Page tokens are kept in a restricted credentials store readable only by the publishing service. Tokens never appear in logs.

Sharing

Not sold and not shared with anyone for their own use. To analyse the owner's content, it is processed on the owner's behalf by the owner's AI assistant accounts (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI Codex); results reach the owner via the owner's Telegram bot and Google Drive. Nothing else.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept only while the owner's authorization lasts and is deleted on request (data deletion).
  • Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook and Instagram access in the Facebook settings under "Apps and websites".

The Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the Meta Platform Terms are followed.
