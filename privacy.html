Swarm Metrics: Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

  • Data accessed: YouTube Analytics reports of the channels whose owner signed in, via the read-only scope yt-analytics.readonly. No other Google data.
  • Use: internal analysis of the owner's own videos only.
  • Storage: on the owner's private computer. Access tokens are kept in memory; refresh tokens in a restricted credentials store.
  • Sharing: none. Not sold, not shared, not used for advertising.
  • Retention and deletion: the owner can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored analytics are deleted on request.
  • The Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, is followed.
  • Contact: socialswarm.ops@gmail.com
